About the series

From Wii-era beginnings to a modern dance service

Just Dance began as a motion-controlled party game and grew into one of Ubisoft’s most recognizable rhythm franchises. The games feature music, choreography, and fun for players of all ages, with a rotating catalog of songs from across decades.

This preview uses the public Just Dance wiki overview as a reference point and presents the core experience in a clean, review-friendly layout.
